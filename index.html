[ 02 ] — PCB assembly process

Every stage feeds the next. Quality is a loop, not a checkpoint.

  1. 01

    Solder paste

    Before components are added, the board is coated with solder paste on the component pads.

  2. 02

    Pick & place

    Machines with reels of components pick each part and place it precisely, programmed straight from the PCB design data.

  3. 03

    Automatic optical inspection

    AOI detects poor joints, misplaced components and, in some cases, the wrong component.

  4. 04

    THT process

    Through-hole components are placed by hand and soldered onto pads on the reverse side.

  5. 05

    Test

    In-circuit test (ICT), functional test and thermal stress test — before anything leaves the factory.
